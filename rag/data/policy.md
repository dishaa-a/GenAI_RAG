# Demo policy handbook

Refunds are available within 30 calendar days after purchase. The customer must provide an order number and proof of payment. Refunds return to the original payment method and usually arrive within five to seven business days after approval.

Standard support hours are Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM India Standard Time. Urgent security incidents can be reported at any time through the incident form. Support does not promise a response time outside business hours.

Personal data is retained for 90 days after account closure unless law requires longer retention. Users can request deletion by contacting privacy@example.test. Backups expire within 30 additional days.

The basic plan includes five team members and 10 GB of storage. The pro plan includes unlimited team members and 1 TB of storage. Plan changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.

To reset a password, use the Forgot password link on the sign-in page. Reset links expire after 20 minutes. Administrators cannot view or recover a user's existing password.
